WELCOME TO KSR ENTERPRISES

Providing Access to RO Purified Drinking Water

KSR Enterprises is a proposed public water ATM and water vending business based in Delhi, India. Our aim is to make RO purified drinking water accessible to the public through convenient water ATM facilities at suitable locations.

We aim to provide convenient water dispensing facilities against payment through multiple payment methods.

ABOUT US

KSR Enterprises aims to develop and operate public Water ATM facilities to provide RO purified drinking water to people.

OUR SERVICES

  1. Public Water ATM

    Water ATM facilities for public use at suitable locations.

  2. RO Purified Drinking Water

    Access to RO purified drinking water through water vending facilities.

  3. Multiple Payment Options

    Payment options may include cash, UPI and other digital methods, depending on the installation.

  4. Water ATM Operations

    Operation and maintenance of company-managed Water ATM facilities.

HOW IT WORKS

  1. Visit an available KSR Enterprises Water ATM.
  2. Select the available water quantity.
  3. Choose an available payment method.
  4. Complete the payment.
  5. Collect water from the dispensing facility.

The actual process and payment options may vary depending on the Water ATM installation.
